Overview

AbleMind is an iOS application developed by BeachDev LLC that connects to Ableton Live via your local network and allows you to control your session using natural language and AI.

We built AbleMind with privacy as a foundation, not an afterthought. The short version: we don't see your prompts, we don't store your session data, and we don't sell anything about you to anyone.

The simple version Your music, your prompts, and your API keys never touch our servers. Everything stays between your iPhone, your computer, and the AI provider you choose.

What We Collect

AbleMind may collect the following limited, anonymous data for the purpose of improving the product:

Data Purpose Identifiable?
App opens / session starts Usage trends No
Feature interactions (e.g. which AI provider selected) Product decisions No
Crash reports Bug fixes No

All analytics data is anonymized and aggregated. We cannot identify you from this data and we do not attempt to.

You can opt out of analytics at any time in the app's Settings screen.

What We Don't Collect

To be explicit about what we do not do:

We never collect Your chat prompts or AI responses · Your Ableton session data (tracks, clips, devices, MIDI) · Your AI provider API keys · Your name, email, or any account information · Your location · Any audio or MIDI you create

BeachDev LLC does not proxy, log, store, or have access to any communication between the AbleMind app and your chosen AI provider. That communication goes directly from your iPhone to the provider's API.

API Keys

AbleMind requires you to provide your own API key from an AI provider (such as Google, xAI, Anthropic, or OpenAI). This is a deliberate design choice.

Your API key is stored exclusively in the iOS Keychain on your device — Apple's encrypted, hardware-protected credential store. It is never transmitted to BeachDev LLC's servers or any third party other than the AI provider you are authenticating with.

How your API key is used Your key is sent directly from your iPhone to your chosen AI provider's API endpoint to authenticate your requests. That is its only use.

AI Providers

AbleMind supports multiple AI providers. When you send a prompt, your message — along with context about your Ableton session — is sent directly from your device to the provider you have selected.

This data is subject to each provider's own privacy policy, not ours. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of your chosen provider:

Provider Privacy Policy
Google (Gemini) ai.google.dev/terms
xAI (Grok) x.ai/legal/privacy
Anthropic (Claude) anthropic.com/privacy
OpenAI (GPT) openai.com/privacy

BeachDev LLC has no visibility into the content of your prompts or the responses returned by these providers.

Analytics

We use minimal, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how AbleMind is being used at an aggregate level. This helps us prioritize features and fix issues.

We do not use third-party advertising analytics or tracking pixels. We do not build profiles of individual users. We do not share analytics data with third parties for any commercial purpose.

To opt out of analytics collection, go to Settings → Privacy → Analytics in the AbleMind app.
