FAQ

Got
questions?

Everything you need to know about AbleMind. Can't find an answer? Reach out directly.

Where do I get LivePhoneBridge and how do I install it?
+
Download LivePhoneBridge from the setup page. Unzip the folder and copy it into your Ableton Live MIDI Remote Scripts directory: ~/Music/Ableton/User Library/Remote Scripts/. Then in Ableton, go to Preferences → Link, Tempo & MIDI → set Control Surface to LivePhoneBridge. Full instructions are included in the download.
What version of Ableton Live do I need?
+
AbleMind works with Ableton Live 10 or later (any edition — Intro, Standard, or Suite). The companion MIDI Remote Script, LivePhoneBridge, is installed manually into your Ableton user library — no third-party installers required.
Does it work over the internet or only on local Wi-Fi?
+
AbleMind connects over your local Wi-Fi network using a WebSocket connection between your iPhone and your Mac running Ableton. Both devices must be on the same network. No internet connection is required for the bridge itself — only for AI API calls.
Do I need an OpenAI account?
+
You'll need an API key from at least one supported provider: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or xAI. You enter the key in AbleMind Settings — it's stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device and sent directly to the provider. AbleMind never sees or stores your key.
What does "unlimited AI commands" mean in Pro?
+
The Free tier has a limited number of AI commands per session to let you try the core experience. Pro removes this cap entirely — send as many commands as you want in a session. Your actual AI costs are covered by your own API key and billed directly by the provider.
Is this available on the App Store?
+
AbleMind is currently in public beta via TestFlight. We're actively developing the app and preparing for an App Store submission. Join TestFlight to get early access and help shape the final release with your feedback.
Can I cancel Pro anytime?
+
Yes — Pro is a standard App Store subscription. You can cancel anytime from your iPhone's subscription settings. Your Pro access continues until the end of the current billing period.
